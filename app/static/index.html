Intended use & limits

  • Portfolio and research demonstration of an end-to-end clinical ML service.
  • Data: UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals, 1999–2008, ICD-9 coded. Practice has changed since.
  • Diabetic inpatients in US hospitals only; not validated elsewhere.
  • Split by patient, so no patient appears in both training and test data.
  • Social factors, follow-up care and adherence are not in the data, which caps accuracy.
